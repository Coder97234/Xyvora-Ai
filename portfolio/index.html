👤 My Story

Where I've been, and where I'm going.

I started making Roblox games because I want to build things people actually play — and turn game development into real income. My first two games are rough, and I'm proud of them anyway: every bug taught me something about scripting, building, and publishing.

Now I want to make bigger, better games — the kind with real polish, more players, and actual earning potential. Right now the thing holding me back is hardware: my current setup can't keep up with Roblox Studio and daily development work.

Next up — my dream project: an all-anime open-world fighting game with characters and moves from across anime, in one shared world. It already has its first signature move designed (see below) — but to actually build it in Roblox Studio every day, I need the right tools. That's the laptop I'm working toward.

SIGNATURE MOVE ● DESIGNED

Hollow Nuke

The character floats up and spins to charge. A blue orb forms in one hand, a red orb in the other — then both are pulled together, and the combined energy detonates in one massive purple explosion. A high-risk, high-reward finisher.

1 · Float & spin 2 · Blue 3 · Red 4 · Pull together 5 · Purple detonation

Why this matters: this move already has a full mechanic breakdown — charge phases, visual effects, timing, and payoff. That's the level of detail I design at. I'm now leveling up my scripting so I can build it exactly as it looks in my head.
