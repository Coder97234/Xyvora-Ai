# Nomination Request — Framework Sponsorship

**Why this matters:** Framework replied to say they *don't* do individual sponsorships — but they **do** run a nomination-based sponsorship program. Someone else nominates you, and their team picks from nominations. This is the only hardware path found so far that doesn't require an audience.

**Nomination form:** https://frame.work/blog/framework-sponsorships

**Who to ask:** anyone adult who has actually seen your work and can speak to it — a teacher (computer science, robotics, media), a school counselor, a librarian, a parent's coworker in tech, a local makerspace lead, a Roblox dev community organizer, a youth program coordinator.

**Key rule:** they must be able to say *something true* about you. A nomination that's vague gets skipped.

## Honest expectation setting

Framework's program is about potential and community contribution, not follower counts — which is genuinely why it fits you better than any of the 40 cold emails. But it's still competitive and not guaranteed. Ask **two or three** people rather than one; multiple nominations describing the same project from different angles is the strongest version of this.
