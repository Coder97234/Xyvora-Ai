# The Story — "A game that can't be built yet"

This is the pitch that works. Not "please send a laptop" — the *story*. It's true, it's specific, and it has a natural ending. Use it everywhere: emails, the Roblox DevForum, a TikTok script, a school application, a sponsorship form.

---

## The short version (30 seconds, say it out loud)

I've been building on Roblox since 2021 and I've published two games. The better one has 115 visits — I'm not going to pretend that's impressive.

But last month I designed a move for my next game that I have never seen run.

It's called Hollow Nuke. The character floats up off the ground and spins slowly while charging. A blue orb forms in one hand, a red orb in the other. Then both hands slam together and it detonates in a purple explosion big enough to cover the screen.

I wrote the whole thing down. Five phases, how long each takes, exactly when an opponent can interrupt it and when they can't, what the particles do, how the code should be structured, why the spin makes it recognizable at a glance.

A complete, working design for something that has never existed on a screen. Because my machine can't handle Roblox Studio well enough to build it every day. So it stays in a document and in my head.

That's the story: a game that's fully designed and can't be built yet. The missing piece isn't ideas or effort — it's one machine.

---

## Why this story works

1. **It's honest about the weak numbers.** Saying "115 visits, I won't pretend that's impressive" buys you credibility instantly. Everyone else inflates.
2. **It has tension.** A designed move that has never run is a real, concrete gap — not "I need a laptop."
3. **It has a payoff built in.** When the machine arrives, the move runs. That's an ending an audience can wait for.
4. **It's specific.** Blue orb, red orb, spin, purple explosion, five phases. Specific beats impressive every time.

---

## How to use it

### As a content series (the strongest use)
**"The Build That Hasn't Happened"**
- Ep 1 — the design document. A move nobody has seen.
- Ep 2 — why it can't run yet (the honest hardware problem).
- Ep 3 — the machine arrives (if it does).
- Ep 4 — **Hollow Nuke runs for the first time, on camera.** The payoff.

Even without Ep 3, Episodes 1 and 2 are real content. Post them now.

### As a Roblox DevForum post
Title idea: *"I designed a 5-phase ultimate for a JJK fighting game — now I need to actually build it"*
Post the phase table, the Luau sketch, and ask for feedback on the interrupt windows. Devs love critiquing mechanics. That thread is more valuable than 100 cold emails.

### As a reply when someone asks for proof
> "I've published two games, neither big. Here's what I'm building next and exactly how it works — including a move I've specced to individual cast timings but haven't been able to build yet. That's what the hardware request is for."

### What NOT to do with it
- Don't claim numbers you don't have.
- Don't say "thousands of players" or "huge community."
- Don't promise reach. Promise work.
- Don't send it twice to the same person without new information.
